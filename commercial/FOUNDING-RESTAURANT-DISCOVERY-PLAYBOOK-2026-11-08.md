# MiseNorth — Founding Restaurant Discovery Playbook
Date: 8 November 2026

## Cohort status
The adjacent JSON file contains ten real Metro Vancouver businesses with public business contact channels and official source URLs. Every record remains `RESEARCHED`, `NOT_CONTACTED`, `DISCOVERY_REQUIRED`; none is a customer. All POS/provider fields remain `NOT_VERIFIED`. The desk-fit score is only a queue-ordering heuristic.

## Positioning for discovery
MiseNorth is an independent restaurant digital operations and guest-growth layer that complements an existing POS. The initial sell is not "replace your POS", "automate your restaurant", or "increase revenue guaranteed". Show a branded menu, QR access, first-party request intake and a more deliberate guest-contact/consent record. Order requests are unpaid requests; no online card processing or live POS sync is included.

## 15-minute discovery structure
1. **Current state (3 min):** How do guests currently find your menu, place pickup orders, join the waitlist or request bookings? Which tools are involved?
2. **Friction (4 min):** Where do staff re-enter information, miss requests, answer repeated questions, or lose direct guest context? Ask for one recent real example and its frequency.
3. **Economics (3 min):** What does the current provider cost? Are marketplace commissions, missed calls, staff minutes or lost repeat business measurable? What is the approximate monthly impact?
4. **Technology boundary (2 min):** Which POS, ordering, reservations, email and loyalty tools are used? Which systems are authoritative? Is a standalone intake/guest layer acceptable?
5. **Willingness to pay (2 min):** Would a C$149 onboarding and C$49/location/month month-to-month offer be worth testing? What exact result must be visible by day 30 to continue?
6. **Next step (1 min):** Agree on a narrow demo using their own public menu structure and a clearly labelled sample tenant. No production guest data or integration credentials during the demo.

## Exit criteria
A prospect moves from RESEARCHED to QUALIFIED only when a person with buying influence confirms:
- a specific recurring problem and its frequency;
- the current alternative and spend/operational cost;
- a clear MiseNorth use case not already solved by the POS or existing stack;
- an acceptable data/security boundary;
- willingness to evaluate the stated price hypothesis;
- a mutually agreed next step and date.

A demo is not qualification. A verbal positive reaction is not a sale. A free account is not an active paid location. ACTIVE_PAID requires a signed agreement, verified subscription/payment state and successful activation.

## Tailored first question by prospect
- **Northern Cafe:** Does phone-based takeout create missed calls or interruptions during peak brunch, and would a first-party pickup request page actually help?
- **Brunch Vancouver:** Is weekday phone reservation / phone pickup friction material, or do the existing third-party channels already cover demand?
- **Kari House:** How many pickup orders arrive by phone versus delivery apps, and would a direct pickup flow be worth a separate channel?
- **Breakfast Table — Steveston:** Does the digital waitlist solve the important guest problem, or is first-party pickup/repeat-guest capture still missing?
- **Fortuna Bakery & Deli:** How are custom cake, platter, bread/deli and wholesale enquiries triaged, quoted and confirmed today?
- **Steveston Bakery and Café:** How much staff time is spent coordinating cake preorders and pickup timing, and where do errors or back-and-forth occur?
- **River Road Cafe:** Is catering/prepared-food pickup a meaningful recurring channel, separate from the frozen-goods shop?
- **Cannery Café:** Is the website/current booking information still maintained, and do guests have a real problem completing menu, booking or order requests online?
- **Minute Cafe:** Does marketplace-led ordering create an unmet first-party repeat-customer or pickup need after fees and customer ownership are considered?
- **Sanctuary Cafe:** Does the informational site adequately serve cycling-route visitors, or do guests ask for menus/preorders that staff currently handle manually?
