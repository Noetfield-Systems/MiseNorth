# MiseNorth — Competitive Intelligence Baseline
Date: 8 November 2026
Geography: Canada / Metro Vancouver
Purpose: initial desk research; verify again during each sales discovery. Provider prices and feature terms change.

## Executive conclusion

A generic digital menu, QR menu, online order page or simple request inbox is not a defensible paid wedge by itself. Square offers a free restaurant tier and online ordering. Relayfy offers a free direct-order tier in the Vancouver market. Eatro makes a branded website available at no charge and monetizes individual modules/transactions. More complete POS suites include online ordering, guest management, marketing or reservations.

The proposed C$49/location/month plus C$149 onboarding is only a pricing hypothesis. It is not yet supported by willingness-to-pay evidence. MiseNorth has to demonstrate measurable incremental value alongside a restaurant's current systems—not claim it replaces them or is already POS-connected.

## Strategic signal

A 19 October 2026 trade-publication interview reports Choice's plan to evolve toward an AI restaurant manager spanning POS, menus, reservations, CRM/loyalty, reviews and marketing. The performance and customer counts are company-reported and not independently verified in this document. It signals that "agentic restaurant operations" is becoming a competitor thesis, not a unique MiseNorth feature.

## MiseNorth's realistic wedge

Keep the first release narrow:
1. Branded, first-party restaurant guest page and live menu.
2. QR access with clear table context.
3. Structured pickup/dine-in order requests and a transparent owner queue.
4. Reservation intake with explicit owner confirmation (not a table allocation engine).
5. Guest request history and consent state.
6. An owner/staff workflow that measurably reduces manual enquiry handling.

Important current limits:
- There is no verified Square, Clover, Lightspeed, Toast or TouchBistro sync yet. MiseNorth can be used adjacent to an existing POS only through manual reconciliation/owner workflows; do not imply orders automatically appear in a POS.
- Guest requests are unpaid; food payments and collected restaurant sales are NOT_TRACKED.
- Reservations are intake + basic cover-overlap checks, not a full floor plan/table assignment engine.
- CRM campaign delivery, unsubscribe/suppression and consent withdrawal are not commissioned.

## Pricing hypothesis — validation rules

The C$149 setup + C$49/month/location hypothesis should only survive if discovery proves:
- the owner has a recurring problem not solved by their current POS/provider;
- the impact can be observed (staff minutes saved, missed requests reduced, better direct guest capture or repeat-visit signal);
- the proposed monthly fee is lower than the value created and lower than the buyer's realistic alternative;
- there is a practical operating flow even without POS synchronization;
- the merchant is willing to pay rather than merely praise a free demo.

For each real prospect capture current provider cost, channel mix, monthly direct orders, manual handling time, missed enquiries, guest repeat-rate baseline, implementation effort and a named buyer. Do not collect sensitive guest records during qualification.

## Confidence and limitations

- This is desk research of public provider pages, not vendor contracts or merchant quotes.
- Currency, promotions, processing fees, eligibility, tax, contract length and country coverage must be confirmed on a live quote before competitive claims are made.
- Pricing snippets for TouchBistro and Choice did not establish a Canadian-dollar denomination; values are preserved without currency conversion.
- No POS provider was inferred for any MiseNorth prospect.
