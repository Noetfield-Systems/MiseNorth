# MiseNorth Terms of Service — Draft for Legal Review

**Status:** Working draft; not approved for publication.  
**Operator:** Noetfield Systems Inc.  
**Contact:** operations@noetfield.com

This is a commercial drafting baseline. It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for counsel-reviewed terms, privacy terms or a signed restaurant agreement.

## 1. Service scope

MiseNorth provides restaurant-facing digital operations features that may include:
- a restaurant-branded public page and digital menu;
- QR menu access;
- direct pickup and dine-in **order requests**;
- reservation requests and staff confirmation workflows;
- tenant-scoped guest and consent records;
- campaign drafting, support and owner/staff tools;
- MiseNorth subscription billing when enabled.

MiseNorth is not represented as a complete point-of-sale system, merchant-of-record service, restaurant payment processor, delivery marketplace, table-allocation engine, inventory system, labour scheduler or tax adviser.

Unless a separately documented integration is approved and verified, MiseNorth does not synchronize orders, menu data, payments or inventory into a restaurant's POS or third-party system.

## 2. Restaurant responsibilities

The restaurant is responsible for:
- accuracy and legality of menu descriptions, prices, allergen disclosures, availability, business details and guest communications;
- confirming applicable tax registration and rates with qualified advisers;
- reviewing, accepting, rejecting and fulfilling order requests;
- reviewing and confirming reservation requests;
- ensuring staff accounts and permissions are appropriate;
- collecting and recording lawful guest-data and marketing consent;
- handling refunds, payments, disputes, receipts and food-service obligations that occur outside MiseNorth;
- maintaining access to its own POS and other authoritative systems.

A request submitted through MiseNorth is not a paid order, a confirmed reservation, a completed visit or a guarantee of restaurant capacity. Guest food payment processing is not currently enabled.

## 3. Accounts and security

Restaurant owners must provide accurate business details, verify their email address and maintain secure credentials. Owners are responsible for authorized users and actions under their workspace, subject to applicable law and the final contract. MiseNorth may suspend access for security, fraud, abuse, unpaid subscription or legal reasons once the relevant procedures are finalized.

## 4. Pricing and payment

The initial founding-partner pricing displayed by MiseNorth is a **commercial hypothesis for discovery**, not a binding offer or live charge until a written order form or approved Checkout flow confirms the plan, price, billing period, tax treatment and terms.

The current hypothesis is C$149 one-time setup and C$99 per restaurant location per month, month-to-month, with no percentage commission on restaurant food sales. Prices may be changed only under approved contract terms.

MiseNorth subscription charges are separate from restaurant guest food payments. Guest food payments, refunds, disputes and restaurant settlements are not processed by the current guest request flow.

## 5. Guest information and marketing

The restaurant is responsible for lawful notices and collection of guest data. Marketing consent is separate from operational processing. MiseNorth records explicit email marketing consent only when the guest opts in; campaign drafts do not send messages. Automated marketing remains disabled until approved sending providers and suppression/unsubscribe controls are active.

## 6. Third-party services

Hosting, billing and communications may rely on third-party providers disclosed in the Privacy Policy and order form. Integrations are active only when authenticated, tenant-scoped and verified. Provider branding or an available integration option does not establish a connected service.

## 7. Availability, support and changes

The service is provided with commercially reasonable operational goals to be defined in the final SLA. Support severity, response targets, maintenance windows, uptime commitments, backup retention and disaster recovery commitments require final approval. No unverified uptime or recovery-time guarantee is made by this draft.

## 8. Data, intellectual property and termination

Restaurant Customers retain rights in their own business content and guest data, subject to third-party rights and law. MiseNorth retains rights in its software, brand and service materials. Export, retention, deletion and post-termination handling must be specified in the final customer agreement and Privacy Policy.

## 9. Disclaimers and liability

Any limitations, exclusions, indemnities, warranty disclaimers, governing-law provisions, dispute resolution, tax and consumer-law terms must be drafted and approved by Canadian counsel. Do not rely on this placeholder section as an enforceable limitation of liability.

## 10. Governing law and execution

The intended initial operating market is Metro Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. Governing law, venue, acceptance, electronic contracting and enforceability must be settled by counsel before publication or contract signature.

**Do not present this draft as an executed agreement or final Terms of Service.**
